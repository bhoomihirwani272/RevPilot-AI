# AGENTAI-035 – SALES MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
## DAY 2 PROGRESS REPORT

### 1. Objective

The main objective of Day 2 was to analyse the generated sales data and develop different analytics modules to understand sales performance, territory performance, quota achievement and deal health.

### 2. Representative Performance Analysis

Created a sales representative scorecard to track the performance of individual sales representatives.

The following details were calculated:
- Total number of deals handled.
- Number of won deals.
- Number of closed deals.
- Win rate.
- Total revenue generated from won deals.

This analysis helps compare the performance of different sales representatives.

**Output:** `data/processed/rep_scorecard.csv`

### 3. Territory Performance Analysis

Developed a territory analytics module to understand how different sales territories are performing.

The following metrics were calculated:
- Total number of accounts.
- Active accounts.
- Account revenue.
- Number of won deals.
- Total won revenue.
- Revenue contribution of the top five accounts.
- Account concentration warning.

This helps identify territories that are performing well and territories that may need more attention.

**Output:** `data/processed/territory_report.csv`

### 4. Quota Attainment Analysis

Implemented quota attainment calculations to compare actual sales with assigned sales targets.

The following calculations were performed:
- Total assigned quota.
- Actual sales.
- Quota attainment percentage.
- Performance status.

The representatives were classified into four categories:

| Attainment | Status |
|---|---|
| 100% or above | Exceeding |
| 70%–99% | On Track |
| 50%–69% | Behind |
| Below 50% | At Risk |

**Output:** `data/processed/quota_summary.csv`

### 5. Sales Pipeline Analysis

Developed a pipeline analytics module to calculate the expected value of ongoing sales opportunities.

Different probabilities were assigned to pipeline stages:

| Stage | Probability |
|---|---:|
| Prospecting | 10% |
| Qualification | 25% |
| Proposal | 50% |
| Negotiation | 75% |
| Closing | 90% |

Weighted Pipeline Value was calculated using:

**Weighted Pipeline = Deal Amount × Stage Probability**

This provides an estimate of the expected revenue from the current sales pipeline.

Generated two files:
- `data/processed/pipeline_details.csv`
- `data/processed/pipeline_summary.csv`

### 6. Deal Health Scoring

Created a rule-based deal health scoring system to understand the condition of individual sales opportunities.

The score was calculated using four factors:

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Stage Progress | 40% |
| Customer Activity | 25% |
| Stakeholder Engagement | 20% |
| Deal Velocity | 15% |

Based on the final score, deals were classified as:

| Score | Category |
|---|---|
| 75–100 | Strong |
| 55–74 | Healthy |
| 35–54 | At Risk |
| Below 35 | Critical |

The average deal health score obtained was **68.28 out of 100**.

**Output:** `data/processed/deal_health_report.csv`
